Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the Azaan 365 mobile application, developed and maintained by Danish Ansari (BeUpdate).

This document explains how user information is collected, used, and protected when using the app.

Information We Collect

To use the app, users are required to create an account. During account creation, we collect the following information:

  • Username
  • Password (stored securely in encrypted form)
  • State (selected by the user)
  • City (selected by the user)

After login, the app may request permission for:

  • Push notifications, used to send Azaan alerts and important app-related updates.

How We Use the Information

The collected information is used only to:

  • Authenticate users securely
  • Display location-based Namaz and Azaan timings
  • Send notifications related to prayers or app updates
  • Improve app stability and user experience

We do not sell, rent, or share personal data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Data Storage and Security

User data is stored securely using industry-standard security practices. Reasonable technical and organizational measures are taken to protect data from unauthorized access, misuse, or disclosure.

Account Deletion

Users can delete their account at any time directly from within the app.

Once an account is deleted, all associated personal data is permanently removed from our systems, except where retention is required by law.

Data Retention

User data is retained only while the account is active. After account deletion, personal data is permanently deleted.

Third-Party Services

The app may use trusted third-party services such as Firebase for push notifications and technical infrastructure.

These services may collect limited technical information as governed by their own privacy policies. We do not control how third-party services handle data.

Children’s Privacy

This app is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page.
