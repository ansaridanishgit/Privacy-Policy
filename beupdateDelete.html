Account Deletion

BeUpdate allows users to permanently delete their account directly from within the application.

How to Delete Your Account

To permanently delete your account:

  1. Open the BeUpdate app.
  2. Go to More.
  3. Open Manage Account.
  4. Select Delete Account.
  5. Tap Delete My Account and confirm.

What Will Be Deleted

When you delete your account, the following information will be permanently removed:

  • Your BeUpdate account and associated account information.
  • Your shop profile and associated shop details.
  • All posts created by you.
  • All product listings created by you.
  • Images and other content associated with your posts and shop.
  • Other data associated with your account.

Permanent Deletion

Account deletion is permanent.

Once your account has been deleted, your account, shop profile, posts, product listings and associated data will be permanently removed from our systems and cannot be recovered or restored.
