Account Deletion

Fast Billing allows users to permanently delete their account directly from within the application.

How to Delete Your Account

To permanently delete your account:

  1. Open the Fast Billing app.
  2. Go to More.
  3. Open Settings.
  4. Select Delete Account.
  5. Confirm the deletion request.

What Will Be Deleted

When you delete your account, the following information will be permanently removed:

  • Your Google Sign-In account association with Fast Billing.
  • Your shop details, including shop name, address, state, and city.
  • All products.
  • All customers.
  • All suppliers.
  • All purchase records.
  • All sales records.
  • All inventory (stock) data.
  • All reports and any other business data associated with your account.

Permanent Deletion

Account deletion is permanent.

Once your account has been deleted, all associated data is permanently removed from our systems and cannot be recovered or restored.
