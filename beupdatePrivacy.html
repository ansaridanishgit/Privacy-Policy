Privacy Policy

BeUpdate · Effective immediately

This Privacy Policy applies to the BeUpdate mobile application, developed and maintained by BeUpdate Official. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it.

Your privacy matters to us. By using BeUpdate, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

1. Information We Collect

BeUpdate uses Google Sign-In for authentication. When you sign in, we receive your basic Google profile:

  • Name
  • Email Address
  • Profile Photo (if available)

You may also provide business information such as:

  • Shop Name
  • Shop Address
  • State
  • City

While using the app, you may create and manage records including: products, customers, suppliers, sales, purchases, inventory (stock), and business reports.

2. Device Permissions

We only request device access when it is needed:

  • Camera & Gallery — only when you choose to add photos to a product or shop you are posting.
  • Location — only if you choose to add your shop's address for nearby discovery.

Browsing and exploring products does not require any of these permissions. We do not require access to your microphone, contacts, storage, or any other sensitive device permissions for core functionality.

3. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used only to:

  • Authenticate your account using Google Sign-In.
  • Maintain your business profile.
  • Manage products, inventory, purchases, sales, and reports.
  • Display your shop/products and help buyers find you.
  • Keep your account secure and improve app functionality.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal or business information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

4. Content Responsibility

You are solely responsible for the content you post. Posting or uploading any political, communal, controversial, obscene, 18+, nude, violent, illegal, criminal, or otherwise offensive content is strictly prohibited and violates our policy and applicable law.

Reporting & Legal Action

If you encounter violating content, report it in-app. Any post found violating our policy will be removed, and the responsible account will be permanently banned.

We also reserve the right to report such content and the user to:

• Cyber Crime authorities (as per the IT Act and applicable Indian cyber laws)

• The user's local police station (as a formal complaint)

• Other relevant legal or regulatory bodies

We fully cooperate with law enforcement.

5. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal and business information from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. However, no online service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Account Deletion & Data Retention

You can permanently delete your account directly from within the application. Once your account is deleted, all associated personal and business data is permanently removed from our systems, except where retention is required by law.

Your data is retained only while your account remains active. After account deletion, your information is permanently removed from our systems unless legal obligations require otherwise.

7. Third-Party Services

BeUpdate uses trusted third-party services such as Google Sign-In and Firebase to provide authentication and backend infrastructure. These services may collect limited technical information according to their own privacy policies.

8. Children's Privacy

BeUpdate is intended for business use and is not directed to children under the age of 13.
