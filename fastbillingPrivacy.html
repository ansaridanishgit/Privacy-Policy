Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the Fast Billing mobile application, developed and maintained by Danish Ansari (BeUpdate).

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your information when you use the Fast Billing application.

Information We Collect

To use Fast Billing, users are required to create an account. During registration, we may collect the following information:

  • Full Name
  • Email Address (if provided)
  • Mobile Number
  • Business Name
  • Password (stored securely in encrypted form)

While using the application, users may also store business-related information, including but not limited to:

  • Customers
  • Suppliers
  • Products
  • Sales Records
  • Purchase Records
  • Inventory/Stock Details
  • Business Reports

The app may request permission for:

  • Push notifications for important account and application updates.

How We Use the Information

The information collected is used to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Manage your business inventory and billing records.
  • Generate reports and analytics.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Improve application performance and user experience.
  • Send important service or account-related notifications.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal or business data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Data Storage and Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your personal information and business data from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

Although we strive to protect your information, no method of electronic storage or internet transmission is completely secure.

Account Deletion

Users can request deletion of their account directly from within the app.

Upon account deletion, all associated personal and business data will be permanently removed from our systems unless retention is required by applicable law.

Data Retention

Your information is retained only while your account remains active. Once your account is deleted, your data is permanently removed from our systems, except where legal obligations require otherwise.

Third-Party Services

Fast Billing may use trusted third-party services such as Firebase for authentication, notifications, analytics, and technical infrastructure.

These third-party providers may collect limited technical information in accordance with their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for their independent privacy practices.

Children's Privacy

Fast Billing is intended for business and commercial use and is not designed for children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy
