Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy applies to the FastBilling mobile application, developed and maintained by FastBilling Official.

This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected when you use the FastBilling application.

Information We Collect

FastBilling uses Google Sign-In for user authentication. When you sign in, we may collect the following information from your Google account:

  • Name
  • Email Address
  • Profile Photo (if available)

After signing in, you may also provide business information such as:

  • Shop Name
  • Shop Address
  • State
  • City

While using the app, you may create and manage business records including:

  • Products
  • Customers
  • Suppliers
  • Sales
  • Purchases
  • Inventory (Stock)
  • Business Reports

Permissions

FastBilling does not require access to your device's camera, microphone, contacts, location, storage, or any other sensitive device permissions for its core functionality.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used only to:

  • Authenticate your account using Google Sign-In.
  • Maintain your business profile.
  • Manage products, inventory, purchases, sales, and reports.
  • Provide and improve the app's functionality and reliability.

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal or business information with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes.

Data Storage and Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your personal and business information from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, or disclosure.

Account Deletion

You can permanently delete your account directly from within the application.

Once your account is deleted, all associated personal and business data is permanently removed from our systems, except where retention is required by law.

Data Retention

Your data is retained only while your account remains active. After account deletion, your information is permanently removed from our systems unless legal obligations require otherwise.

Third-Party Services

FastBilling uses trusted third-party services such as Google Sign-In and Firebase to provide authentication and backend infrastructure.

These services may collect limited technical information according to their own privacy policies.

Children's Privacy

FastBilling is intended for business use and is not directed to children under the age of 13.
