Delete Account – Azaan 365

This page explains how users of the Azaan 365 mobile application can request deletion of their account and associated data.

App Name: Azaan 365

Package Name: com.azaan365appdaksh

Developer: Danish Ansari (BeUpdate)

How to delete your account

Users can permanently delete their account directly from within the app by following the steps below:

  • Open the Azaan 365 app
  • Log in to your account
  • Go to Profile / Settings
  • Select Delete Account
  • Confirm using your password

Once confirmed, your account and all associated data are permanently deleted from our systems.

What data is deleted

  • User profile information
  • Account credentials
  • App-related activity data linked to the account

No personal data is retained after account deletion, except where required by law.
